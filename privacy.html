Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Grand Billionaire City RP. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us while participating in our community, utilizing our web services, or connecting via our Discord server.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you interact with our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect minimal information necessary to provide a secure and stable roleplay environment:

  • Discord Account Data: Publicly available profile details (username, user ID, avatar) when you authenticate or join our Discord server.
  • In-Game Data: Character names, economy records, faction status, and gameplay telemetry stored securely on our game servers.
  • Log Data: Standard connection metadata, IP addresses, and diagnostic information required for server security and anti-cheat enforcement.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • Managing user permissions, roles, and whitelist verification on our Discord and game servers.
  • Enforcing community guidelines, rules, and security measures to prevent abuse.
  • Improving server performance, stability, and developing new roleplay features.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security protocols to protect your data from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with third-party services such as Discord and web hosting providers. These services operate under their own independent privacy policies, and we encourage you to review their respective terms.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our administration team directly through our official Discord Server.
