Frequently Asked Questions

How do I recover my lost character?
If your account was linked to Google Play, Apple ID, or Discord, simply log in using the same platform on your device. If your account was a guest account, please submit a ticket below with your In-Game Username and approximate Player Level.
I bought currency but didn't receive it. What should I do?
Purchases can take up to 10 minutes to process depending on server load. If your items do not appear after restarting the app, submit a billing ticket below with your Store Order ID (from Apple or Google Play).
What are the minimum system requirements?
Grand Varexis requires a minimum of 3GB RAM, Android 8.0+ or iOS 13.0+, and an active internet connection for stable 60 FPS gameplay.
