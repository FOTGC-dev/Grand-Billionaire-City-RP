Frequently Asked Questions

Join our Discord server first — that's where you'll find the connection details, community guidelines, and any whitelist steps you'll need to complete before jumping in.
Whitelisting details and requirements are posted in our Discord server. Check the relevant channel there for the current process and status.
Open a ticket in our Discord server and our staff team will review your case. Please include your character name and any relevant details.
Use the dedicated reporting channel in our Discord server. Include as much detail as possible — screenshots or clips help our team resolve things faster.
Refund requests are handled on a case-by-case basis. Reach out through our Discord server with your order details and our team will assist you.
Applications for staff positions and factions are posted and managed through our Discord server. Keep an eye on the announcements channel for open recruitment.
